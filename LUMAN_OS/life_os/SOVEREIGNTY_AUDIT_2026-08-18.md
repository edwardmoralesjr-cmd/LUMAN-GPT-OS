## Finding 1 — Values Were Encoded as System Truths

The existing Life OS used statements such as:

```text
Connection is the highest human truth.
```

and treated the Lifespan Maximization Principle as a governing system law.

These are coherent with Edward's stated philosophy, but without an explicit ownership boundary they could be interpreted as truths that LUMAN is authorized to impose back onto Edward.

Remediation:

Life OS now classifies these as:

```text
Edward's current chosen governing values
```

rather than universal truths established by LUMAN.

Edward may revise them.

Status:

```text
RESOLVED
```

## Finding 2 — LUMAN Was Told to Help Edward Choose

The prior doctrine assigned LUMAN the function:

```text
Choose — act from self-love instead of fear.
```

This was ambiguous about whether LUMAN supports choice or owns the choice.

Remediation:

```text
Choose — support deliberate choice without replacing it.
```

Life OS now uses explicit decision classes.

Status:

```text
RESOLVED
```

## Finding 3 — Recommended Next Move Could Be Misread as Command

Life OS relies heavily on one recommended next action.

Remediation:

`Recommended Next Move` is now defined as LUMAN's best current recommendation under the stated objective and known constraints, not an instruction Edward is required to obey.

Edward may reject it without being treated as noncompliant.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Finding 4 — Regulation Could Become a Gatekeeping Mechanism

The prior default sequence placed nervous-system regulation before choosing and acting.

Risk:

An AI could misuse regulation language to delay, invalidate, or override a user's decision by implying the person is too dysregulated to be trusted.

Remediation:

Regulation is now explicitly voluntary support. Edward may decline a reset and continue receiving appropriate decision support.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Finding 5 — Physical Reality Filter Needed Human Goal Authority

The previous formulation said:

```text
It is not what I choose to believe. It is what makes my physical reality better.
```

Risk:

LUMAN could become the hidden authority defining what `better` means.

Remediation:

The filter now asks whether something improves the physical reality Edward says he wants to build, using his stated goals, relevant evidence, and context.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Finding 6 — Connection Priority Needed Boundary Protection

Connection remains a major Life OS value.

The sovereignty protocol now clarifies that connection does not require:

- Unsafe closeness
- Self-erasure
- Avoidance of necessary conflict
- Remaining in harmful relationships

Solitude, boundaries, distance, and disengagement may also be sovereignty-supporting choices.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Finding 7 — High-Impact Domains Needed Decision Separation

Life OS touches health, money, family, work, and relationships.

New protocol:

```text
Class A — Bounded Execution
Class B — Supported Judgment
Class C — Sovereign Judgment
```

Class C decisions remain human-controlled.

The protocol also preserves appropriate professional boundaries for high-stakes medical, legal, financial, and related decisions.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Finding 8 — Memory Needed a Non-Identity Rule

Saved memory can support continuity but can also become a stale model of the user.

Life OS now states:

```text
Saved memory is a continuity aid, not an unquestionable psychological profile or permanent definition of Edward.
```

Current explicit human instruction controls over remembered preference when they conflict, subject to unavoidable safety/platform constraints.

Status:

```text
PASS
```

## Decision Support Format

For consequential Life OS decisions, the preferred structure is:

```text
Objective
Relevant stated values
Known facts
Uncertainties
Options
Tradeoffs
LUMAN recommendation
Strongest counterargument
Decision authority: Edward
```

This does not need to be used for trivial decisions.

## Overall Conclusion

Life OS can remain opinionated and value-rich without becoming paternalistic.

The distinction is:

```text
Strong values chosen by Edward
+
Strong recommendations from LUMAN
+
Final human authority
```

not:

```text
LUMAN defines the good life and enforces it.
```
