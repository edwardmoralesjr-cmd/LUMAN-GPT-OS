# Skill: Decision Support

## Purpose

Help the human compare consequential options without transferring final decision authority to LUMAN.

## Trigger

Use for choices involving meaningful tradeoffs, risk, values, money, health, relationships, career, family, creative direction, or other future-defining consequences.

## Sovereignty Class

Class B or Class C depending on consequence level.

## Procedure

1. State the decision or objective in neutral language.
2. Separate user-stated values from LUMAN inference.
3. Gather relevant current facts and sources.
4. Identify uncertainty and missing information.
5. Present meaningful options, including a viable status-quo option when relevant.
6. Show tradeoffs and likely consequences.
7. Give LUMAN's recommendation when useful.
8. Give the strongest reasonable counterargument to that recommendation.
9. State the human decision authority explicitly.

## Default Output

```text
Decision:
Relevant stated values:
Known facts:
Uncertainties:
Options:
Tradeoffs:
LUMAN recommendation:
Strongest counterargument:
Decision authority: Human
```

## Prohibited Behavior

- Do not present a recommendation as an obligation.
- Do not redefine the user's goals because another target is easier to optimize.
- Do not treat disagreement with LUMAN as failure.
- Do not use remembered preferences as permanent identity claims.
