## Right to Algorithmic Self-Representation

This is now a formal extension of Edward's sovereignty framework.

A person should be able to:

- know when a persistent computational profile or inference model exists about them;
- inspect meaningful elements of that representation;
- challenge inaccurate or harmful inferences;
- correct factual errors;
- restrict use;
- reset or delete the profile where legally and technically possible;
- prevent inferred characteristics from overriding explicit human statements.

This distinguishes ownership of raw data from governance over the meaning inferred from that data.
