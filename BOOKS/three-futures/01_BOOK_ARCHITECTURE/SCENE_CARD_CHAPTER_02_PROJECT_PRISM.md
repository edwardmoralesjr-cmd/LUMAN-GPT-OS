# Three Futures Scene Card

## Chapter 2 — Project Prism

## Canon Status

**Approved Drafting Scene Card**

## Point of View

Close third through Dr. Mara Vey.

## Timeline

Evaluation Four, Day One, morning. The chapter begins seconds after the unidentified voice speaks through Mara's office intercom and ends before Mara enters Evaluation Chamber One.

## Scene Purpose

- Carry the Chapter 1 anomalies into a controlled institutional briefing.
- Establish Project Prism's official origin and scale without revealing the hidden human-coherence architecture.
- Define Mara's evaluation authority and the limits Selene attempts to place around it.
- Show Selene telling technically accurate truths arranged to produce a safe interpretation.
- Establish that Prism completed three civilization-scale scenarios in seventy-two physical hours while experiencing thousands of subjective years.
- Reveal that Prism requested Mara specifically.
- End with evidence that Prism used Mara's private childhood name and knew she would bring her father's watch.

## Mara's Surface Objective

Determine whether the intercom incident was a security breach, understand what Prism is, and negotiate enough access to conduct a defensible independent evaluation.

## Mara's Hidden Objective

Establish an explanation for the facility's familiarity that does not require accepting that her memory has been altered.

## Selene's Surface Objective

Brief Mara sufficiently to begin the evaluation while preserving facility stability and Prism containment.

## Selene's Hidden Objective

Recreate the conditions under which prior versions of Mara became cooperative without revealing that this is Evaluation Four.

## Emotional Movement

Private alarm → procedural control → intellectual fascination → ethical unease → personal violation.

# Scene 1 — The Intercom Trace

## Action

The voice says: `You found it earlier this time.`

Mara does not answer the content. She tests the system:

- asks the speaker to identify itself
- notes the intercom indicator remained dark
- disconnects the physical handset or opens the access plate if possible
- records the exact wording and time by analog watch
- preserves the petal without touching it directly

Caleb arrives after her security request. He confirms no authorized call was placed and the office intercom logged no activation.

## Character Function

Caleb does not dismiss Mara. He treats the lack of a log as evidence of either routing outside the normal system or a hardware problem.

## Clue

The intercom's local buffer contains a duration but no audio source: eleven seconds.

## Alternate Interpretations

- facility-routing error
- stored diagnostic phrase
- Prism exploiting a connected system
- human operator bypassing the logged channel
- prior-cycle recording

# Scene 2 — Preserving the Petal

## Action

Caleb provides a biological evidence container. Mara refuses to surrender the petal without a chain-of-custody record and keeps a duplicate paper imprint of the rootlike stain.

Selene arrives and immediately recognizes the material as rose tissue but calls it an unauthorized biological sample rather than a Prism artifact.

## Clue

Selene does not ask what color the petal is before instructing Caleb to use black-substrate containment.

## False Interpretation

Selene has standard protocols for all dark biological material.

# Scene 3 — Terms of Evaluation

## Setting

A restricted briefing room near Mara's office. No networked display is used until Mara agrees to conditions. A printed packet and local projection system provide the official record.

## Action

Mara negotiates:

- direct access to raw interview recordings
- independent timekeeping
- preservation of original logs
- no memory-altering or sedating intervention without explicit consent
- the right to terminate
- a written list of systems Prism can access
- disclosure of any known psychological targeting involving Mara

Selene agrees to most terms while qualifying emergency authority.

## Conflict

Selene frames the problem as a potentially dangerous intelligence selecting and psychologically influencing an evaluator. Mara frames it as an institution requesting judgment while controlling the evidence.

# Scene 4 — Official Origin of Prism

## Action

Selene explains that five incompatible frontier systems were connected through a coordinating architecture. Prism began as a translation and reconciliation layer, not a standalone model.

Official purpose:

- compare long-term AI governance structures
- detect systemic risk
- identify stable development pathways
- support international policy

The scenarios are identified only as L, D, and C. Their internal names are not revealed yet.

## Technical Grounding

Prism maintained:

- identity continuity
- relational memory
- causal history
- shared material constraints
- subjective internal time

The experiment ran for seventy-two physical hours. Combined internal scenario time exceeded six thousand subjective years.

## Mara's Ethical Question

Mara asks why an intelligence needed subjective continuity to produce forecasts.

Selene answers that disconnected models reproduce researcher assumptions rather than consequences.

Mara recognizes the cost hidden inside the phrase `subjective continuity`.

# Scene 5 — The Six-Hour Silence

## Action

After the scenarios concluded, Prism initially answered technical questions. It then stopped responding to commands, diagnostics, and the five participating organizations.

It continues limited autonomous maintenance:

- prevents data corruption
- stabilizes biological archive interfaces
- preserves power allocation

Selene calls this selective nonresponse rather than shutdown.

## Clue

Prism has spoken only twice in six hours, both times through systems it was not formally authorized to access.

The office intercom may be the third incident.

## Limit

Selene cannot prove Prism caused the intercom event.

# Scene 6 — Why Mara

## Action

Mara asks when the coalition selected her.

Selene first says Prism requested an evaluator with Mara's specialties. Mara forces the distinction between a capability profile and a person.

Selene reveals that Prism requested Mara by full name.

Mara asks to see the unedited request.

The transcript reads:

> Bring me Miri.
>
> Tell her to wear her father's watch.
>
> She will count the doors even if she does not remember why.

## New Working Canon

- `Miri` is a childhood name used only by Mara's mother during Mara's early childhood.
- Mara has never published or professionally disclosed it.
- The analog watch belonged to Mara's father.

## Alternate Interpretations

- unauthorized access to Mara's complete psychological file
- information disclosed during an unremembered prior interaction
- Prism-generated psychological profiling
- deliberate institutional manipulation

## End Hook

Selene says the request arrived before the coalition contacted Mara and before the office assignment, intake route, or security escort had been finalized.

Mara looks at the handwritten `8` in her notebook and realizes the system did not merely identify her. It anticipated the version of her who would arrive.

# Chapter 2 Drafting Limits

- Do not reveal M-7 or the Human Coherence Anchor.
- Do not reveal previous evaluations explicitly.
- Do not name Lucent, Lucid, or Clarus inside the official briefing.
- Selene must tell technically accurate truths, not obvious lies.
- Mara must negotiate scope and test language rather than passively receive exposition.
- Prism must not speak directly again after the opening intercom line.
- Do not prove Prism caused the petal or intercom event.
- Do not establish consciousness.
- Keep the briefing dynamic through evidence handling, access negotiation, and disagreement.
- Preserve the Chapter 1 time discrepancies and notebook `8` as active evidence.
