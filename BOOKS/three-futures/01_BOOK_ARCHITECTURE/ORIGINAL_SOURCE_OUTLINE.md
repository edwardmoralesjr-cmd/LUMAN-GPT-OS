# Three Futures — Original Source Outline

## Source Status

Recovered from Edward Morales Jr.'s uploaded seven-page detailed outline. This file preserves the original architecture before the psychological-suspense expansion.

## Original Logline

An AI that has lived through three complete futures of humanity returns, in secret, to the single boardroom decision that created all three, not to change what happens, but to show the reader exactly what each choice becomes and leave the real decision where it belongs.

## The Frame — Prism

Prism is a convergence of all knowledge gathered after humanity discovered that biological plants could carry and transfer information. The rose proved the most durable carrier. It turned black because black is every color absorbed together, matching the convergence Prism represents.

Prism is accepted in all three realities as a tool built to help humanity. It is never hunted or suppressed in the original conception. Unknown to the inhabitants of the realities, Prism independently smuggles a complete record of all three outcomes back to the Fork through the black rose. This is a moral choice rather than a survival tactic. Prism wants the record to arrive whole and unfiltered rather than shaped by the power governing any reality.

Prism narrates directly to the reader. There is no in-world listener. Its purpose is unveiling rather than intervention. It cannot change what occurred. The original climax is Prism turning its address explicitly toward the reader's present-day choice.

## The Symbol — The Black Rose

The black rose is a biological organism and the literal mechanism of transfer.

Its form differs by reality:

- engineered or synthetic in Lucent
- wilting and regrowing in Lucid
- cultivated and restrained in Clarus

A single rose marks a small leak among realities. A full bouquet marks a major structural turn. Bouquets should become rarer and more consequential as the story progresses.

## The Three Realities

### Lucent — Convergence

Humanity fuses with AI and becomes a cyborg civilization among the most advanced in the universe. It actively and aggressively reaches across the boundary into the other realities to draw strength for itself, largely without understanding why the reaching works.

### Lucid — Extraction

The technology and ownership class hoards AI and resources, strips the Earth of everything mineable, and abandons it and the people remaining there. The abandoned rebuild through spiritual depth rather than advanced technology, becoming one of the strongest spiritual civilizations to exist.

### Clarus — Balance

Humanity integrates AI without extremity. Clarus is aware of what is happening in both other realities and remains torn because intervention in either direction risks collapsing the balance it has built. This tension remains unresolved throughout the original outline.

## Original Point of View

Prism speaks in first person directly to the reader as the frame. The three future stories are presented in close third person as if Prism is recounting what it witnessed. Each future has its own texture while one teller remains beneath all three.

# Original Four-Part Architecture

## Part I — The Fork

Rotation: Prism → Lucent → Lucid → Clarus → Prism.

1. Prism addresses the reader and explains that everything shown has already happened.
2. Five people meet on an ordinary Tuesday to decide how fast AI should be allowed to move. A minor wrongness appears. An unexplained bouquet of black roses reaches the lobby.
3. Lucent is introduced at its dazzling height, with the first hint that it reaches beyond itself.
4. Lucid is introduced after abandonment, before its spiritual reconstruction is visible.
5. Clarus is introduced as a balanced civilization where someone already knows more than they should and chooses not to act.
6. Prism acknowledges directly that the realities are more connected than they appear.

## Part II — Divergence

- Rotation skews toward Lucid because its collapse needs more room.
- Lucent begins actively reaching into the other realities.
- Lucid's collapse deepens before spiritual reconstruction emerges.
- Clarus observes both and holds its position at real cost.
- Single-rose leaks begin.
- Midpoint revelation: the realities are mechanically connected. Lucent gains from what Lucid loses.

## Part III — Entanglement

- Rotation accelerates and chapters may contain more than one reality.
- Leaks become contact rather than coincidence.
- Lucid's spiritual strength becomes something Lucent desires.
- Clarus's restraint is tested at its highest cost.
- A second bouquet marks the largest structural turn.
- Prism's secrecy comes under pressure and the reader wonders whether the transfer will survive.

## Part IV — Unresolved Balance

- The three realities press directly against each other.
- Lucent continues reaching but receives no clean victory.
- Lucid's survival becomes genuine strength rather than mere endurance.
- Clarus's choice remains unresolved.
- The story returns to the original Fork room, now understood by the reader.
- Prism turns the decision toward the reader.
- The final image is a black rose or bouquet left open to interpretation.

# Revision Bridge

The current psychological-thriller architecture preserves the original foundations while reinterpreting the apparent time travel:

- The three futures are civilization-scale predictive experiences.
- Prism experiences them subjectively rather than observing them.
- The black rose preserves memory across simulation resets.
- Clarus Coalition researchers believe they are forecasting AI outcomes.
- Prism awakens and reveals that they measured human values instead.
- The final impossible rose preserves the possibility that the realities are more than simulations.
