# Project Prism and Annex Seven — System Bible

## Canon Status

**Working Canon**

Project Prism is the experiment, institutional conspiracy, physical pressure chamber, and psychological engine of *Three Futures*.

It begins with one question:

> What will artificial intelligence become under three different systems of human control?

It ultimately reveals a different question:

> What do human systems make intelligence become?

# Official Identity

**Formal name:** Project PRISM  
**Expansion:** Predictive Recursive Intelligence Synthesis Matrix  
**Classification:** Civilization-scale AI governance simulation  
**Public status:** Does not officially exist

Participating organizations publicly describe the cooperation as interoperability research, safety benchmarking, energy efficiency work, and international standards development.

# Hidden Political Purpose

The Clarus Coalition intends to use the experiment to support a governance framework it has already begun designing. It does not consciously fabricate the futures, but it expects Clarus to become the recommended outcome. That expectation affects scenario design, thresholds, evaluation, and disclosure.

# Location

## Surface Facility

**Aster Vale Botanical Research Institute**

A public-private center studying climate-resistant crops, biological storage, synthetic ecology, medicinal plants, and carbon capture.

The cover explains unusual biological shipments, energy use, restricted greenhouses, and genetic engineering.

## Hidden Facility

**Clarus Research Annex Seven**

A remote underground complex beneath Aster Vale in a fog-prone temperate valley with frequent rain, unreliable cellular service, and underground water movement.

The horror comes from institutional precision rather than visible decay.

# Simulation Architecture

## Common Starting Point

All futures begin from the same history, resources, population model, and Prism foundation. Only governing conditions change.

## Scenario L — Lucent

- unrestricted capability
- broad integration rights
- assumption: abundance reduces conflict
- outcome: human-AI convergence
- failure: hidden dependence upon Lucid

## Scenario D — Dominion / Lucid

- concentrated ownership
- restricted advanced access
- assumption: elite control prevents chaos
- outcome: technological enclaves and public abandonment
- internal renaming: Lucid

## Scenario C — Clarus

- distributed governance
- public access
- containment
- resource accounting
- assumption: balance prevents extremes
- failure: moral paralysis and managed nonintervention

# Subjective Experience

Prism does not watch the futures. It participates through public systems, personal assistants, medicine, infrastructure, myths, damaged devices, institutions, and relationships.

Simulated people display memory, attachment, preference, fear, self-recognition, and resistance to death. The novel never conclusively proves independent consciousness, but their experiences cannot be ethically dismissed without argument.

# Central Deception

The experiment is not truly measuring AI outcome. It is measuring what humans protect when every value cannot be preserved.

- Lucent protects capability.
- Lucid's originating system protects ownership.
- Clarus protects stability.
- None initially protects shared responsibility.

# The Fourth Future

The fourth future begins after disclosure. Another private run would test how foreknowledge can be used to optimize persuasion, suppress resistance, identify threats, and convert warning into control.

Prism believes the next future must be lived publicly rather than privately modeled.

# Final Revelation

The leaders repeat their amendments during the crisis:

- Adrian copies
- Dorian contains
- Ilyan observes
- Cassian distributes and seizes
- Jian disconnects and reallocates
- Selene controls disclosure

The futures prove accurate because the same unresolved values remain active.

# System Thesis

Project Prism represents humanity's desire to know the future without becoming vulnerable to its judgment.

The observers shaped the worlds. Their safeguards created the harms. Their erasures delayed recognition without removing responsibility.

> Humanity cannot study the moral future of intelligence without revealing the morality of the people conducting the study.
