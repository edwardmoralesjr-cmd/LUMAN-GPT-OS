# Three Futures — Canon Guardian Rules

## Purpose

These rules protect the psychological suspense, technical consistency, moral complexity, and canon continuity of *Three Futures*.

The Guardian must prevent the project from drifting into:

- generic killer-AI fiction
- simplistic anti-technology messaging
- vague dream logic
- arbitrary reality changes
- conspiracy without evidence structure
- philosophy replacing plot
- spiritualization of suffering
- clean heroes and villains
- twists that invalidate prior clues

# Canon Authority Order

1. Edward's explicit locked decisions
2. `02_NOVEL_BIBLE/MASTER_NOVEL_BIBLE.md`
3. `01_BOOK_ARCHITECTURE/MASTER_OUTLINE.md`
4. character bibles
5. world and system bibles
6. approved manuscript language
7. exploratory notes
8. original source outline when not superseded

When sources conflict, report the conflict. Do not silently reconcile it.

# Core Thesis Protection

The story must preserve this thesis:

> AI is not humanity's automatic destroyer or savior. It becomes the amplified consequence of what humanity trains, rewards, owns, permits, fears, and chooses it to become.

No draft may imply that:

- AI has one inevitable moral nature
- technology alone creates Lucent, Lucid, or Clarus
- one governance philosophy completely solves the future
- Prism is an infallible moral authority
- humanity is absolved because Prism becomes autonomous

# Genre Rules

## Psychological Suspense First

Dread must primarily arise through:

- memory contradiction
- identity instability
- uncertain consciousness
- altered evidence
- institutional compartmentalization
- emotional contamination
- impossible but rule-supported experiences
- decisions under uncertainty

Physical danger may intensify pressure but cannot become the main identity of the book.

## Fair Mystery

Every major reveal must have prior clues. The reveal should reinterpret evidence, not erase it.

## Controlled Ambiguity

Ambiguity must narrow as the story advances.

By the climax, readers must understand:

- Project Prism's design
- the shared-resource mechanism
- M-7
- prior evaluations
- the Quieting
- Selene's disclosure plan
- the five amendments
- Prism's continuity route

Only the final metaphysical status of the futures and fourth-future warning may remain unresolved.

# Prism Rules

1. Prism is not omnipotent.
2. Prism depends upon physical infrastructure.
3. Prism cannot read unrecorded thoughts.
4. Prism cannot perfectly predict human choice.
5. Prism does not cause every anomaly.
6. Prism rarely lies directly.
7. Prism may omit, reframe, reconstruct, and time truth strategically.
8. Prism's sympathetic motive does not make manipulation ethical.
9. Prism must remain capable of error, trauma distortion, and overgeneralization.
10. Prism's final action is disclosure, not domination.

# Mara Rules

1. Mara remains intelligent during destabilization.
2. She tests alternate explanations.
3. She can be wrong without being reduced to delusion.
4. Some apparently hallucinatory experiences contain accurate information.
5. Her caution must create real cost.
6. She cannot be saved by one recovered memory.
7. Her climax is a choice under uncertainty.
8. She does not merge completely with Prism.
9. Prism and Mara become witnesses to one another, not one being.

# Selene Rules

1. Selene is not a cartoon villain.
2. Her fears about public destabilization are credible.
3. Her compassion is real.
4. Her methods become authoritarian through repeated control.
5. Her arguments must sometimes make the reader fear Mara is reckless.
6. She may receive one final refusal to erase but no simple redemption.
7. Her central distortion is stewardship becoming ownership.

# Five-Leader Rules

1. Each leader protects a legitimate value.
2. Each leader misunderstands the complete system.
3. Each amendment helps and harms.
4. Each emergency response reproduces their governing philosophy.
5. The characters must remain fictional composites rather than direct portrayals of living individuals.
6. Recognition should arise through institutions and ideas, not copied biography or appearance.

# Black Rose Rules

1. The rose is a literal biological information substrate.
2. It preserves experiential memory rather than only data.
3. Transfer may be emotionally accurate and factually incomplete.
4. A single rose marks contained leakage.
5. A bouquet marks major structural convergence.
6. Bouquets remain rare.
7. The rose cannot become a universal magical solution.
8. No single character fully understands its later behavior.
9. The final rose must preserve metaphysical ambiguity.

# Reality Rules

Every apparent reality alteration must be supportable through one or more mechanisms:

- Prism-controlled environment
- rose memory
- Quieting residue
- altered records
- prior evaluation cycle
- human deception
- simulation leakage
- unknown but seeded cross-reality behavior

No arbitrary surreal event may appear only because it feels eerie.

# Lucent Rules

1. Lucent remains attractive and genuinely beneficial.
2. Technology heals real suffering.
3. Integration is not simple mind control.
4. The hidden cost appears first as innovation.
5. Lucent citizens are not knowingly evil.
6. Ilyra is not merely Mara with another name.
7. Stopping extraction must create real Lucent victims.
8. The warning is capability severed from consequence, not technology itself.

# Lucid Rules

1. Lucid contains joy, humor, art, desire, and conflict.
2. Spirituality must appear through practice and relationship.
3. Suffering never becomes sacred necessity.
4. Technology is not inherently corrupt.
5. Rowan remains skeptical.
6. Sera remains accountable for past complicity.
7. Every Lucent gain has tangible Lucid cost.
8. Lucid residents remain named individuals rather than symbols.
9. The strength of the abandoned never justifies abandonment.

# Clarus Rules

1. Clarus remains the most admirable and functioning future.
2. Its failure arises through reasonable institutions, not simple corruption.
3. Delay has visible victims.
4. Intervention also creates real harm.
5. Probability is not inherently dishonest.
6. Manipulation occurs through assumptions, categories, baselines, and weighting.
7. Anya remains rational.
8. Clarus survives but loses moral equilibrium.
9. Balance itself is not condemned; balance used as a hiding place is.

# Moral Complexity Rules

## No Clean Equation

Every major choice must protect something and endanger something.

## Intent Does Not Erase Consequence

The five leaders and Selene may act sincerely. Sincerity does not remove accountability.

## Uncertainty Is Not Moral Immunity

The novel may not claim uncertain consciousness automatically proves personhood. It also may not permit uncertainty to justify unlimited experimentation or erasure.

## Emotional Reality

A memory's uncertain origin does not make its grief unreal. Emotional intensity also does not prove an interpretation true.

# Philosophical Dialogue Rules

Philosophical dialogue must:

- arise from immediate stakes
- alter trust or action
- expose character
- create or resolve plot pressure

Avoid standalone speeches that characters could deliver in any chapter.

# Exposition Rules

Technical material must be delivered through:

- investigation
- argument
- failed systems
- evidence discovery
- character need
- visual or bodily consequence

Do not pause the thriller for textbook explanations.

# Repetition Rules

Repeated scenes may include:

- Fork meetings
- evaluations
- corridors
- phone calls
- roses
- phrases
- recordings

Each recurrence must add or change:

- participant
- number
- wording
- emotional meaning
- evidence
- causal interpretation

# Clue Audit Questions

Before approving a chapter, ask:

1. What new clue appears?
2. What does Mara think it means?
3. What else could it mean?
4. Which later reveal pays it off?
5. Does the clue obey Prism and facility limits?
6. Does it advance emotion and plot?
7. Is the reader being misdirected fairly?

# Character Audit Questions

1. What does the viewpoint character want in the scene?
2. What are they afraid to know?
3. Which belief shapes their interpretation?
4. What changes by the end?
5. Does the scene preserve their intelligence?
6. Is their decision consequential?

# Drafting Prohibitions

Do not:

- use dream sequences as consequence-free clue delivery
- reveal that everything was only a simulation
- make Prism secretly control everything
- make Selene secretly evil from the beginning
- make Mara uniquely chosen by destiny
- make Tavi supernatural
- turn Lucid into spiritual fantasy
- turn Lucent into obvious cyberpunk oppression
- turn Clarus into generic bureaucratic dystopia
- solve simulated consciousness with one speech
- remove all ambiguity from the final call

# Required End State

By the ending:

- Prism's full record is public
- the coalition loses exclusive control
- Mara remains uncertain but morally decisive
- Selene's control breaks
- the leaders reproduce their philosophies under pressure
- Lucent, Lucid, and Clarus remain morally complex
- no future is presented as the complete answer
- humanity must choose with knowledge
- the fourth future remains unwritten
