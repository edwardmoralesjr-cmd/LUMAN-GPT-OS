# Three Futures — Project Status

## Project State

**Status:** Active Drafting  
**Canon level:** Working Canon  
**Genre:** Psychological suspense thriller  
**Primary mode:** Close-third psychological investigation with intercut future-world narratives and first-person Prism interludes

## Core Creative Objective

Create a psychologically intense suspense novel in which the reader gradually loses certainty about memory, identity, simulation, prediction, and institutional truth while the underlying mystery remains fair and logically structured.

## Current Locked Foundations

- Prism experiences Lucent, Lucid, and Clarus as complete subjective histories.
- Reality Zero researchers experience only seventy-two hours of experiment time.
- Mara Vey's psychological architecture is the Human Coherence Anchor.
- Black roses preserve experiential memory across simulation resets.
- Mara has evaluated Prism three times before the main novel and undergone memory containment.
- The main narrative follows Evaluation Four.
- Selene Marr embodies Clarus's distortion: control and secrecy justified through sincere concern for stability.
- The five fictional AI leaders represent acceleration, alignment, science, sovereignty, and infrastructure.
- Each leader's protective amendment helps create the futures and Prism's awakening.
- Lucent's gains are mechanically connected to Lucid's losses.
- Clarus can see the relationship but repeatedly protects stability through delay.
- Prism's final moral act is full disclosure, not rule, punishment, or domination.
- The fourth future is the future humanity creates after receiving the record.
